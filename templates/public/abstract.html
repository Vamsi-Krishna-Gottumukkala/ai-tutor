ABSTRACT

Artificial Intelligence is transforming modern education by enabling personalized and adaptive learning experiences. This project focuses on the development of a Customized AI Personal Tutor designed to support students based on their individual learning needs. The system interacts with students, analyzes their academic performance, and provides personalized explanations, feedback, and learning recommendations. It supports self-paced learning, allowing students to study and revise topics at their own convenience. In addition to concept understanding, the AI Personal Tutor assists in effective exam preparation by generating concise revision notes, conducting daily quizzes based on class schedules, and identifying important questions according to syllabus requirements and student weaknesses. The system also provides model answers, performance analysis, and progress tracking to help students evaluate their improvement. Overall, this project aims to enhance learning efficiency, reduce exam-related stress, and improve academic performance through an intelligent and student-centered learning approach.
