ℹ About the Application

The AI Personal Tutor provides personalized learning using Artificial Intelligence. It analyzes student performance and gives smart recommendations to improve learning.

⚙ How It Works

  • Attempt quizzes
  • AI analyzes performance (accuracy, time, attempts)
  • Provides feedback and suggestions
🎯 Why It Matters

  • Improves learning speed
  • Helps in exam preparation
  • Gives personalized guidance
📊 Features
  • AI Tutor
  • Quizzes & Assessments
  • Progress Tracking
  • Skill Level Detection
